# Media and screenshot notes

## Included artwork

- `assets/blockside-cover.svg` — repository/site hero illustration.
- `assets/home-preview.svg` — illustrative dashboard preview.
- `assets/blockside-icon.svg` — scalable brand icon.

These are original vector illustrations and design previews. They are **not photos or screenshots of a running iPhone, iPad, or Minecraft game**. Do not label them as captured app screenshots or gameplay.

## Capture genuine screenshots

After installing and launching a compatible build on a physical iPhone/iPad:

1. Enable JIT if needed and make sure the app has finished initial setup.
2. Capture screens using the device's Side + Volume Up buttons (or the device's screenshot shortcut).
3. Capture the dashboard, version selection, real world list, and gameplay only where those screens are available and working.
4. Avoid showing email addresses, account identifiers, access tokens, server addresses, or private worlds.
5. Save original PNG files under `docs/screenshots/` and add a short note with device model, iOS version, app build/tag, and whether the image was edited.
6. Do not use screenshots from another launcher or images that suggest Mojang/Microsoft endorsement.

## Release/social artwork

The cover SVG is 1200×630 and suitable as a repository social preview. The app icon SVG is scalable. For stores or social networks that require PNG/JPEG, export from the SVG at their requested dimensions and check the result at phone size before posting.
